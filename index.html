You're standing on 1F of Taipei Main Station, phone in hand. Your friends just arrived but they're lost somewhere in B1.

You open LINE and start an audio call. "Where are you?" you ask.

Your voice becomes tiny packets of data leaving your phone...

The packets float through the station, traveling from 1F down to B1...

"I'm near the Breeze food court!" your friend replies. The packets made it.

Simple, right? Your voice just floated through the air to their phone. ...except that's NOT how it works at all.
