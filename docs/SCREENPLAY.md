# Screenplay — Scene 1: Naive Mental Model

```
SCENE: NAIVE MENTAL MODEL — TAIPEI MAIN STATION
An 8-bit pixel-art cross-section. The station hums with life.
Ambient trains loop through the MRT and THSR tracks.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────

STEP 0 — [Wide shot, full station]
"This is Taipei Main Station. Four floors of organized chaos —
trains, shops, and 500,000 people passing through every day."

  The camera holds wide. Both characters are tiny figures
  in the vast cross-section. Trains pass through.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────

STEP 1 — [Camera zooms to You on 1F]
"You're on 1F, phone in hand. Your friend just arrived in
Taiwan for the first time..."

  Camera zooms 3x to "You" — blue shirt, phone raised.
  A pulsing highlight rectangle draws attention.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────

STEP 2 — [Camera pans down to Friend on B1; speech bubble appears]
"...and they're lost somewhere in B1, two floors below."

  Diagonal pan to Friend on B1. Green shirt, also holding phone.
  A speech bubble with crying emoji floats above their head,
  gently bouncing. The distance between You and Friend
  is palpable.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────

STEP 3 — [Camera zooms to Your phone; screen glows]
"You open LINE and start an audio call."

  Tight zoom (4.5x) on Your phone. The screen glows brighter —
  LINE call connecting. The moment of action.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────

STEP 4 — [Packets spawn from phone]
"Your voice becomes tiny packets of data, leaving your phone..."

  Five colored packets emerge from the phone, staggered.
  Red, blue, green, yellow, pink — each a tiny 4px square
  with a white highlight pixel.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────

STEP 5 — [Camera follows packets; trains swoosh past]
"...racing through the station, past MRT trains and
concrete walls..."

  Camera tracks the lead packet at 3.5x zoom. The packet
  path crosses the MRT zone — a blue train SWOOSHES
  right-to-left, filling the viewport. Then through B1,
  and an orange THSR bullet train BLASTS left-to-right.
  Packets fly in front of the trains, creating depth.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────

STEP 6 — [Camera arrives at Friend's phone; packets land]
"'I see the Breeze sign!' your friend says.
The packets made it."

  Camera settles on Friend's phone (4x zoom). Packets
  arrive one by one. Phone screen glows. Connection
  established.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────

STEP 7 — [Camera zooms out; scene dims]
"Simple, right? Your voice just flew through the building.
...except that's NOT how it works at all."

  Dramatic zoom-out to wide shot. A dark overlay fades in.
  Red text: "...or is it?" The reveal — everything you
  just saw was the naive mental model.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────

                      END SCENE 1
```
