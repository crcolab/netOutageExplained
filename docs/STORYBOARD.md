# Storyboard — Scene 1: Naive Mental Model (Taipei Main Station)

## Shot 1: Establishing Wide
- **Progress**: 0.000 → 0.060
- **Camera**: `{ x: 160, y: 200, zoom: 1.0 }`
- **Visual**: Full station cross-section visible. Ambient MRT and THSR trains loop through tracks. Both "You" (1F, blue) and "Friend" (B1, green) are tiny figures. Ceiling lights glow. The station hums with life.

## Shot 2: Zoom to You
- **Progress**: 0.060 → 0.120
- **Camera**: `{ x: 64, y: 95, zoom: 3.0 }`
- **Visual**: Smooth zoom to "You" standing on 1F. A pulsing highlight rectangle draws attention. Phone in hand, screen dimly lit. 1F pillars and ceiling lights frame the shot. The station sign is just visible at the top edge.

## Shot 3: Pan to Friend
- **Progress**: 0.120 → 0.160
- **Camera**: `{ x: 244, y: 225, zoom: 3.0 }`
- **Visual**: Diagonal pan sweeps down-right across the MRT tracks to B1. The camera settles on "Friend" — green shirt, also holding a phone. A pulsing highlight appears. The distance between the two characters is felt viscerally.

## Shot 4: Speech Bubble
- **Progress**: 0.130 → 0.200
- **Camera**: `{ x: 244, y: 225, zoom: 3.0 }` (hold)
- **Visual**: A white pixel-art speech bubble fades in above Friend's head. Inside: a crying emoji face (dot eyes, blue tears, open mouth). The bubble gently bounces 1–2px vertically. Emotional beat — Friend is lost.

## Shot 5: Zoom to Phone
- **Progress**: 0.200 → 0.250
- **Camera**: `{ x: 72, y: 90, zoom: 4.5 }`
- **Visual**: Tight zoom on Your phone screen. The phone glow intensifies — LINE call connecting. The screen fills a large portion of the viewport at this zoom level.

## Shot 6: Packets Spawn
- **Progress**: 0.250 → 0.280
- **Camera**: `{ x: 72, y: 90, zoom: 4.5 }` (hold)
- **Visual**: Five colored packets (red, blue, green, yellow, pink) appear staggered at the phone position. They begin their journey upward. The camera holds steady as they emerge.

## Shot 7: Follow Packets + MRT Pass-by
- **Progress**: 0.280 → 0.450
- **Camera**: Tracks lead packet position, `zoom: 3.5`
- **Visual**: Camera follows the lead packet as it floats up and across the ceiling. As packets descend past the MRT track zone (~progress 0.35–0.45), a dramatic MRT train swooshes **right-to-left** across the frame. At zoom 3.5, the train fills a huge portion of the viewport — very cinematic.

## Shot 8: Follow Packets + THSR Pass-by
- **Progress**: 0.450 → 0.650
- **Camera**: Tracks lead packet position, `zoom: 3.5`
- **Visual**: Packets continue descending through B1 toward the THSR zone. At progress 0.52–0.60, an orange THSR bullet train blasts **left-to-right** at high speed. The tighter progress range makes it feel faster than the MRT. Packets fly in front of the train, creating depth.

## Shot 9: Arrival
- **Progress**: 0.650 → 0.700
- **Camera**: `{ x: 252, y: 222, zoom: 4.0 }`
- **Visual**: Camera settles on Friend's phone. Packets land one by one. Friend's phone screen glows brighter. A moment of connection — the call works.

## Shot 10: Zoom Out
- **Progress**: 0.700 → 0.800
- **Camera**: `{ x: 160, y: 200, zoom: 1.0 }`
- **Visual**: Dramatic pull-back to full station view. The entire path the packets traveled is now visible — a tiny distance in the real world. Both characters visible again as tiny figures.

## Shot 11: The Twist
- **Progress**: 0.800 → 1.000
- **Camera**: `{ x: 160, y: 200, zoom: 1.0 }` (hold)
- **Visual**: Scene dims with a dark overlay. Red text fades in: "...or is it?" The implication: this naive model is wrong. Transition to the real explanation.
